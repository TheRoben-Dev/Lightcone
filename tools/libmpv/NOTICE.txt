Lightcone's playback engine: libmpv
===================================

Lightcone (MIT licence) plays media through libmpv, which ships as a separate shared library
next to the app (libmpv.so.2, libmpv-2.dll or libmpv.2.dylib) and can be swapped for another
build of libmpv (client API 2.0 or newer).

That library is built from unmodified sources by tools/libmpv/build.sh in the Lightcone
repository; the exact versions are listed in libmpv-versions.txt next to it. It contains:

  mpv          LGPL 2.1 or later (built with -Dgpl=false)     https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv
  FFmpeg       LGPL 3 or later (built with --enable-version3)  https://ffmpeg.org
  libplacebo   LGPL 2.1 or later                              https://code.videolan.org/videolan/libplacebo
  libass       ISC                                            https://github.com/libass/libass
  FreeType     FreeType Licence (BSD-style)                   https://freetype.org
  HarfBuzz     MIT ("Old MIT")                                https://harfbuzz.github.io
  FriBidi      LGPL 2.1 or later                              https://github.com/fribidi/fribidi
  Mbed TLS     Apache 2.0 (Linux and Windows builds)          https://github.com/Mbed-TLS/mbedtls
  zlib         zlib Licence (Windows build)                   https://zlib.net

As the LGPL requires, the complete corresponding source is available: every component above is
fetched at the pinned tag or checksum in tools/libmpv/versions.env, and build.sh reproduces the
library from them. To use your own libmpv instead, replace the file, or point the
LIGHTCONE_LIBMPV environment variable at it.
